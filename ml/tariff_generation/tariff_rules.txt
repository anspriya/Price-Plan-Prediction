Tariff Plan Generation and Benchmark Pricing

The tariff planning system is designed to generate usage-based telecom tariff plans from historical customer calling behavior. The objective is to create tariff tiers that reflect different customer usage patterns while providing a consistent benchmark for pricing.

1. Customer Usage Analysis

Historical call records are analyzed based on major usage categories:

* Day minutes
* Evening minutes
* Night minutes
* International minutes

Customers with similar usage behavior are grouped into clusters. Each cluster represents a distinct usage profile, such as high-volume, moderate, or time-specific usage patterns.

2. Cluster-Based Tariff Construction

After clustering, the usage distribution within each cluster is analyzed using statistical measures such as percentiles, quartiles, median, and mean.

These statistics help identify different levels of customer demand within a cluster. Based on these usage levels, multiple tariff tiers are constructed with different minute allowances.

The tariff allowances therefore represent usage levels observed in the underlying customer population rather than arbitrary values.

For example, a tariff tier may provide a defined allowance for Day, Evening, Night, and International minutes. Lower tiers target relatively lower usage levels, while higher tiers provide larger allowances for customers with greater usage.

3. Benchmark-Based Pricing

Once the minute allowances are established, each tariff plan is assigned a benchmark monthly price using standard Century telecom benchmark rates.

The benchmark rates used are:

| Usage Category | Benchmark Rate |
| -------------- | -------------: |
| Day            |     $0.170/min |
| Evening        |     $0.085/min |
| Night          |     $0.045/min |
| International  |     $0.270/min |

The monthly benchmark price is calculated as:

Monthly Benchmark Price =

`(Day Minutes × 0.170) + (Evening Minutes × 0.085) + (Night Minutes × 0.045) + (International Minutes × 0.270)`

For example, consider a tariff tier with:

* 237 Day minutes
* 253 Evening minutes
* 254 Night minutes
* 20 International minutes

The benchmark price is calculated as:

* Day: 237 × $0.170 = $40.29
* Evening: 253 × $0.085 = $21.505
* Night: 254 × $0.045 = $11.43
* International: 20 × $0.270 = $5.40

Therefore:

Monthly Benchmark Price = $78.63

4. Interpretation

The resulting price represents a benchmark/reference value for the tariff plan. It provides a consistent monetary basis for comparing plans with different usage allowances.

The overall process can therefore be summarized as:

Historical Usage → Customer Clustering → Usage Distribution Analysis → Tariff Allowances → Benchmark Rate Calculation → Monthly Benchmark Price**

This approach enables the system to generate differentiated tariff plans based on actual customer usage patterns while maintaining a transparent and reproducible pricing methodology.
